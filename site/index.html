Rules & Scoring

Upper Section

Scores the sum of matching dice for that face (1s through 6s).

Bonus: If upper sum is 63 or more, score +35 bonus.

Lower Section

  • 3 of a Kind: At least 3 matching dice. Scores sum of all dice.
  • 4 of a Kind: At least 4 matching dice. Scores sum of all dice.
  • Full House: 3 of one face and 2 of another. Scores 25 points.
  • Small Straight: 4 sequential dice. Scores 30 points.
  • Large Straight: 5 sequential dice. Scores 40 points.
  • Yahtzee: 5 of a kind. Scores 50 points.
  • Chance: Any combination. Scores sum of all dice.
  • Extra Yahtzee: +100 bonus for each additional 5 of a kind after scoring 50 in Yahtzee.
